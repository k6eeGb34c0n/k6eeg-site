About K6EEG

K6EEG is an amateur radio station in San Francisco's Excelsior District. The beacon is an experiment in combining a propagation beacon with a useful, automatically generated bulletin for radio amateurs and emergency communicators.

Contact: k6eegbeacon@gmail.com. Reception reports, comments, and suggestions are welcome.
