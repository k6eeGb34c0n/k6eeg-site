Working an activator

  • Keep it short: your call, a signal report, and your state or park (park-to-park contacts give your park reference).
  • On VHF FM, listen on 146.52 MHz (2 m) and 446.0 MHz (70 cm), the national simplex calling frequencies; SSB activators often sit near 144.200 and 432.100 MHz.
  • Activators need 10 contacts for a valid POTA activation and 4 for SOTA points, so a contact from you can make their day.
  • Other programs worth watching: World Wide Flora and Fauna (WWFF), Islands on the Air (IOTA), lighthouse weekends (ILLW, August), and VHF contest rovers.
