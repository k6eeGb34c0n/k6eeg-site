Audio test schedule

One test each hour, in a 6-hour cycle that starts at 0000, 0600, 1200 and 1800 UTC. Each test runs for the whole hour shown: it plays at 25 and 55 minutes past the hour (for example 0125Z and 0155Z for the audio sweep), in open air after the short update. Pacific times are shown in PDT; they shift by an hour when daylight saving time starts or ends.
