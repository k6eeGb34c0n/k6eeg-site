This is a strong M-class flare, enough for an R2 radio blackout, with HF signals fading for tens of minutes on the sunlit side of Earth. Data from NOAA.

Flares black out HF on the daylight side of Earth. Try night-side paths or wait it out. Blackouts usually last minutes to an hour.
