Latest solar flare: class M1.3. This is a medium-size flare, enough for an R1 radio blackout, with brief HF fadeouts on the sunlit side of Earth. Data from NOAA.

Flares black out HF on the daylight side of Earth. Try night-side paths or wait it out. Blackouts usually last minutes to an hour. Coronal holes become more common as the cycle declines, bringing geomagnetic activity that repeats about every 27 days, one solar rotation. If conditions are disturbed today, watch for a repeat in about four weeks.
