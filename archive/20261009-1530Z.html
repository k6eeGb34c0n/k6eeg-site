Latest solar flare: class M1.3. This is a medium-size flare, enough for an R1 radio blackout, with brief HF fadeouts on the sunlit side of Earth. Data from NOAA.

Flares black out HF on the daylight side of Earth. Try night-side paths or wait it out. Blackouts usually last minutes to an hour. The declining phase of the solar cycle often brings some of the cycle's strongest flares and geomagnetic storms, so VHF aurora openings on 6 and 2 meters are still very possible.
