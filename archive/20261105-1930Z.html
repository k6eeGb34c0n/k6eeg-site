This is a medium-size flare, enough for an R1 radio blackout, with brief HF fadeouts on the sunlit side of Earth. Data from NOAA.

Flares black out HF on the daylight side of Earth. Try night-side paths or wait it out. Blackouts usually last minutes to an hour. As the cycle declines, 10 and 12 meter openings become shorter and less frequent. Winter daytime openings on 10 through 15 meters will hold up longest.
