This site is experimental and preliminary: use its contents with caution. A 24-hour voice beacon from San Francisco on UHF, narrowband FM, frequency to be announced, reporting the conditions radio amateurs care about: space weather and HF propagation, tropospheric ducting, satellite passes, DX activity, weather, tides, air quality, and earthquakes. It will also be a propagation beacon: its steady carrier will tell listeners around the Bay Area and beyond how 33 centimeters is getting out.
