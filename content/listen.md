# How to Listen

**Frequency:** to be announced. **Not on the air yet.** The beacon is in bench testing and isn't transmitting with an external antenna. A frequency survey (listening for existing users) and frequency coordination come next. It will most likely operate in narrowband FM on 33 centimeters (902 to 928 MHz) and/or 23 centimeters (1240 to 1300 MHz); the frequency will be announced here once it's coordinated.

## The schedule

| Minutes past the hour | What airs |
| --- | --- |
| :00 and :30 | Full report: operations, then space at :05, Earth at :10 |
| :15 and :45 | A one-minute update: maximum usable frequency, DX, satellites, and anything new |
| In between | The steady carrier, with status bulletins, CW messages read back by voice, and CW IDs |

Reports in the first half of each hour use US units; the second half uses metric.

## Equipment

Many scanners and some ham handhelds and mobiles cover 902 to 928 MHz. Converted commercial 900 MHz
radios work well, and so does any software-defined radio receiver. A simple vertical or small Yagi helps
a great deal at this frequency.

## Reception reports

Reports help map how 33 centimeter signals travel. Please send your location, equipment, antenna,
and what you heard to **k6eegbeacon@gmail.com**.
