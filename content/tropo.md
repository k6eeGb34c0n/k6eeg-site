# Experimental Tropo Forecast

The beacon's tropo forecast turns NOAA's global weather model into a **chance of ducting on 2 meters,
70 centimeters, and 23 centimeters**, like a rain forecast. The chances were calibrated against a year
of weather-balloon soundings at Oakland, San Diego, Quillayute, Lihue, and Hilo, and they check out:
when the forecast says 80 percent or more, balloons found a duct over 90 percent of the time.

| Region | How well it works |
| --- | --- |
| California coast | Strong: clearly separates good days from bad |
| Pacific Northwest coast | Moderate: useful, especially on 70 cm |
| Hawaii | Ducts are nearly always present there, so the forecast adds little |

The model can say how likely ducting is, but not reliably at what height; the balloon soundings in the
beacon's TROPO section give heights. For wide-area and multi-day views, see the
[Hepburn tropo maps](https://www.dxinfocentre.com/tropo.html) and [F5LEN](https://tropo.f5len.org/).
