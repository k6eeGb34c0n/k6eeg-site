Space · 10:35 AM PDT · 3:41

Space weather: solar flux 131, 15 above its 90-day average of 116. That's moderately high. Maximum usable frequency 26 megahertz, measured by the Idaho Falls ionosonde via KC2G: paths within North America open through 15 meters, 12 meters marginal, 10 meters and up above the regional MUF. Critical frequency 7.5 megahertz, so 80 and 60 meters are good for NVIS, 40 meters marginal. The MUF is higher toward South America, about 35 megahertz, so 10 meters may open on that path. Daytime absorption limits 160 and 80 meters to short range. K index 1, very quiet. Data from NOAA.

Lower solar flux means the maximum usable frequency drops, so 20 meters becomes the main daytime DX band, and 40 and 80 meters do more of the work at night.
