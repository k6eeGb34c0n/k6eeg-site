Space · 12:05 PM PDT · 4:14

Space weather: solar flux 90, 20 below its 90-day average of 110. Maximum usable frequency 27 megahertz, measured by the Idaho Falls ionosonde via KC2G: paths within North America open through 15 meters, 12 meters marginal, 10 meters and up above the regional MUF. Critical frequency 8.8 megahertz, so 80, 60, and 40 meters are good for NVIS. The MUF is higher toward South America, about 35 megahertz, so 10 meters may open on that path. Daytime absorption limits 160 and 80 meters to short range. K index 3, active. No significant flares. Data from NOAA.

The declining phase of the solar cycle often brings some of the cycle's strongest flares and geomagnetic storms, so VHF aurora openings on 6 and 2 meters are still very possible.
