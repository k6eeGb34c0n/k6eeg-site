Space weather: solar flux 125, 15 above its 90-day average of 110. That's moderately high. Maximum usable frequency 11 megahertz, measured by the Idaho Falls ionosonde via KC2G: paths within North America open through 40 meters, 30 meters marginal, 20 meters and up above the regional MUF.
