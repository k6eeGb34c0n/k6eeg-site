Space weather: solar flux 93, 22 below its 90-day average of 115. Maximum usable frequency 24 megahertz, measured by the Idaho Falls ionosonde via KC2G: paths within North America open through 15 meters, 12 meters marginal, 10 meters and up above the regional MUF. Critical frequency 7.0 megahertz, so 80 and 60 meters are good for NVIS, 40 meters marginal. The MUF is higher toward South America, about 32 megahertz, so 10 meters may open on that path. Daytime absorption limits 160 and 80 meters to short range. K index 2, quiet. No significant flares. Data from NOAA.

As the cycle declines, 10 and 12 meter openings become shorter and less frequent. Winter daytime openings on 10 through 15 meters will hold up longest.
